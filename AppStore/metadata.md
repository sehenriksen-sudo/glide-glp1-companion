## Description

```
Finally, an app that understands your GLP-1 journey.

Glide tracks your medication, weight, food, and workouts — then shows you exactly what's happening inside your body so you can make the most of every dose.

─────────────────────────────
KNOW YOUR CONCENTRATION
─────────────────────────────
Glide plots a real-time concentration curve for every injection of Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, or Zepbound. See exactly how much medication is active in your system today — and when your next shot is due.

Watch your Live Activity update on the lock screen all week long.

─────────────────────────────
PREDICT THE FOOD NOISE
─────────────────────────────
Food noise — the constant mental chatter about eating — gets louder as your medication wears off. Glide calculates your personal vulnerability window and warns you before cravings hit, so you can plan ahead instead of react.

─────────────────────────────
TRACK EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
─────────────────────────────
· Injection log with dose history and concentration chart
· Weight log with progress toward your goal
· Nutrition log with protein, carbs, and fat macros
· Exercise log synced directly from Apple Health
· Dashboard that ties it all together at a glance
· Home screen widgets — small, medium, and large

─────────────────────────────
CONNECT WITH THE COMMUNITY
─────────────────────────────
You're not alone. Glide's anonymous community chat connects you with other GLP-1 users at the same stage of their journey — no names, no judgment, just shared experience. Every conversation is ephemeral and private.

Your health data never leaves your device. Medication logs, weight entries, and meal data are stored entirely on-device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Glide never uploads your medical profile to external servers.
```
